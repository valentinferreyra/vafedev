## Context

At Mercado Libre, I worked on real-time notifications for customer service representatives. Notifications could display announcements to individual users, teams, or large audiences.

## My contribution

I worked on both frontend and backend functionality. The implementation used KVS, Streams, and Jobs. MySQL supported creating teams so notifications could target a team rather than requiring individual recipients.

## Delivery at scale

For notifications directed at many users, we used batched delivery to control outgoing traffic and reduce the risk of HTTP 429 responses or service disruption.

## Stack

KVS, Streams, Jobs, and MySQL.
